Grooming & Attire Check

How to use it. You have not missed anything — this is written for someone who has never opened it.

You can practise on this page. There is a working copy of the app below with made-up students in it. Tapping anything in it is safe: nothing you do there is saved, and nobody sees it.

1

What this is

It is where you record what was called out at a grooming check, while the student is still standing in front of you.

It replaces the stack of paper that used to be typed up afterwards. Class tutors still tick their own namelists as they always have — this is only for the students sent to the front.

About five seconds a student, most of it deciding rather than waiting.

2

How to open it

There is nothing to install. It is a web page — you open it the same way you would open any website, in the browser you already have on your phone.

sajc-discipline-comm.github.io/grooming-and-attire-check Tap this to open it. There is no App Store, nothing to download, nothing to update.

It is worth putting it on your home screen so it sits with your other apps and you never have to find the link again.

Put it on my home screen — iPhone
  1. Open the link above in Safari. It has to be Safari for this to work.
  2. Tap the share button at the bottom of the screen — the square with an arrow pointing up out of it.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, top right.

It now sits on your home screen like any other app.

Put it on my home screen — Android
  1. Open the link above in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen. Some phones say Install app — either is the right one.
  4. Tap Add, or Install.

It now sits on your home screen like any other app.

3

How to sign in

Two things: your name, and the password Kim gave you. Then tap Sign in.

Put your own real name in. It is saved next to whatever you record, so the committee knows who did the checking — that is all it is for.

After you sign in it spends two or three seconds fetching the list of students. That wait happens once. Everything after it is instant, so sign in before you are standing in front of anybody, not while.

If you close the tab, you sign in again next time. Leaving it open in the background is fine.

4

How to log a student

Four taps, and then it repeats. Find them → check it is the right person → tap what was called out → save.

Try it now. The frame below is a working copy with invented students in it. Type art in the box, or just a couple of letters, and follow it through. You cannot do any harm.

The same thing in words

  1. Find them. Type any part of the name — two or three letters is usually enough — and tap them in the list. Typing a class code brings up that whole class.
  2. Check it is the right student. Their name and class are shown large. If it is not them, tap No — go back. Never wave one through.
  3. Tap what was called out. As many as apply. Nothing is recorded yet.
  4. Tap Save. Then Next student, and you are back at the search box.

Thirty students in a row without ever leaving that loop.

5

How to undo a mistake

Everybody saves the wrong thing eventually. It takes one tap to take it back, and it only ever removes that one student — never anybody you did before them.

Straight away: on the screen that says Saved, tap That was wrong — undo.

A few students later: tap Finish for now to get back to the front screen. Under Entered this session are the last ten students you recorded. Tap one to take it back.

Practise both here — the frame starts on the Saved screen.

More, when you need it

You do not need any of this to do the job. It is here for the day something is not obvious.

Scanning a student card instead of typing

On the search screen, tap 📷 Scan their card instead. Fit the whole card inside the box on screen, make sure the name is sharp, and press the round button.

It reads the name off the photo, so the reading is only ever as good as the picture. If it comes back with several possible students, it is telling you it is not certain which one — read them properly and pick the right one. It is built to ask rather than guess, because a grooming record filed against the wrong student is not caught by anything later.

If it cannot read the card at all, tap Type it and search for the name. That is not a workaround; it is there for exactly this.

The recheck list — when students come back

Days later, students come back to show they have fixed something. Tap Recheck list on the front screen.

Two ways to sort it, one tap apart. By class when you are calling names out. By priority when you are chasing — the ones who did not turn up last time come to the top.

Tap a student, then tap each item they have fixed. Anything you do not tap stays open, so you can clear one thing and leave another. If they were called and did not appear, tap Didn't show up — that marks them and pushes them up the list.

📋 Copy the list puts the whole list on your clipboard as plain text, ready to paste into a message.

Remarks — the box under the offences

Optional. It is for when the category does not quite cover what you saw — thickness rather than length, that sort of thing. Leave it empty the rest of the time.

The name will not come up

Almost always a spelling difference. Try a different part of the name — a middle name, or the last word of it — or type their class code to bring up the whole class and find them by eye.

Only if they are genuinely not there, tap Record anyway — not in the roster yet. "Roster" is just the list of students' names. That button is for a student who has transferred in and has not been added to the list yet. It records them under the name you typed, marks it so somebody can tidy it up afterwards, and nothing is lost.

The amber messages, and what they mean

"Already open: Hair Colour" — on the confirm screen. This student already has that offence outstanding from a previous check. The button underneath changes to Yes — recheck them, and tapping it takes you to their recheck rather than a fresh check. Nothing is wrong.

"No Student ID — recorded for linking up later" — you used Record anyway for someone not on the list. It is a note to whoever tidies up, not a problem for you.

Amber in this app never means you have broken something. It means: notice this.

Worth knowing
  • It needs internet. With no signal it says so plainly rather than pretending to save.
  • Nothing is stored on your phone. Close the tab and it is gone from the phone. The record itself lives in the committee's spreadsheet.
  • Two of you can use it at the same time. You will not overwrite each other, and your undo can never remove somebody else's student.
  • You cannot break it by tapping around. Nothing is recorded until you press Save.
  • Checked the same student for the same thing twice? It refuses, and says so. There is simply nothing to add.
  • Somebody edited the student list while you were working? ↻ Refresh the roster on the front screen pulls the list of names again.
It will not work at all

If the page opens but nothing you do goes through — it will not sign you in, or it says it cannot reach the records — that is on the spreadsheet side, not yours. Nothing you did caused it and nothing you can tap will fix it.

Tell Kim, and say what you were doing when it stopped. There is a check she can run that says which of three things it is, and it is usually a two-minute fix.

One more thing

If you print this page, you get a single sheet with just the loop, the undo, and the what-if-it-goes-wrong list. Good for the staffroom drawer.
