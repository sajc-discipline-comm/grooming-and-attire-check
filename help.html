Grooming & Attire Check

Instructions and tutorial.

There is a practice copy of the app below with made-up students in it. Tapping anything in it is safe and is meant for your own exploration.

1

What this is

This app is used to record students live when you are checking their grooming and attire at the front. It is intended to log students quickly and easily refer to them in the future when they need to be called for rechecks.

If you are asking: "Where's the excel sheet?" It's with Kim. But the goal is that with this app, you would have very little need to open it.

2

How to open it

Kim will give you the link.

sajc-discipline-comm.github.io/grooming-and-attire-check It is a web page. Nothing to install.
Keep it on your home screen

iPhone. Open the link in Safari → tap the share button at the bottom (square with an arrow out of it) → scroll down → Add to Home Screen → Add.

Android. Open the link in Chrome → tap the three dots, top right → Add to Home screen (some phones say Install app) → Add.

3

How to sign in

Type in your name or initials, and the password given by Kim.

It will take approx. 5 seconds to load the roster — the list of student names — and after that you are ready to go.

4

How to log a student

  1. Type any part of the student's name, or their class.
  2. Tap on the student. Check if it is the right student. If wrong, press No — go back.
  3. Tap on the offences they need to be registered for.
  4. Tap Save. Then Next student, if you still have students after this current one.

How to undo

  1. Just saved it? Tap That was wrong — undo.
  2. A few students later? Tap Finish for now, then under Entered this session tap the student to take it back.

It only ever removes that one student. Try both below.

6

How to recheck

When students come back to show they have fixed something.

  1. Tap Recheck list on the front screen.
  2. By class for calling names out. By priority to chase the worst first.
  3. Tap the student when they come forward.
  4. Tap each item they have fixed. Anything you don't tap stays open.
  5. Tap Save. If they never turned up, tap Didn't show up instead.

FAQ
Why is there no excel sheet?

There is one, and it is with Kim. Everything you tap goes straight into it.

The point of the app is that you should not need to open it.

How did you make this?

Kim built it. It is a web page that writes straight into a Google spreadsheet owned by the committee's own account.

Nothing is kept on your phone — close the tab and it is gone from there. Two people can use it at the same time without overwriting each other.

What's "scan a student card" for?

For when you don't know the name. Instead of typing, photograph their card and it reads the name off it, then you pick them from what it found.

Fit the whole card in the box and make sure the name is sharp. If it can't read it, tap Type it and search instead — that is what it is there for.

Grooming & Attire Check

A web page — nothing to install. Kim has the link. Sign in with your name or initials and the password Kim gave you. About 5 seconds to load, then you're ready.

Logging a student

  1. Type any part of the name, or their class.
  2. Tap the student. Check it is the right one — if not, No — go back.
  1. Tap the offences they need registered.
  2. Save, then Next student.

Undoing

  • Just saved it — tap That was wrong — undo.
  • A few students later — Finish for now, then tap the student under Entered this session. Last ten.
  • Longer ago — tell Kim.

Rechecking

Recheck list on the front screen. By class for calling out, by priority to chase. Tap the student, tap each item they have fixed — anything untapped stays open — then Save. Never turned up: Didn't show up.

If something is odd

  • Card won't scan — tap Type it and search the name.
  • Several students offered — it isn't certain which. Read them and pick.
  • Name won't come up — usually a spelling difference. Try another part of the name, or the class.
  • Amber "Already open: …" — they still have that offence outstanding. Nothing is wrong.
  • Nothing works at all — tell Kim what you were doing when it stopped.

Nothing is recorded until you press Save · nothing is stored on your phone · two people can use it at once · you cannot break it by tapping around.
